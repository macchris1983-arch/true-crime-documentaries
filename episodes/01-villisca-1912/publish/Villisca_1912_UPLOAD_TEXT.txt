TITLE
The Villisca Axe Murders: The File That Kept Growing (1912–1931)

DESCRIPTION
In June 1912, eight people were killed in a house in Villisca, Iowa: Joe and Sarah Moore, their four children, and two young guests, Lena and Ina Stillinger. No one was ever convicted.

The usual story says the case was lost in the first hours, when a crowd walked through the house. This film argues the opposite: for nineteen years the file kept growing. A closet. A photograph said to come from a child's eye. A sound that turned into a man. A signed confession. A fingerprint. This is an inventory of what was in the house, and what was carried in afterward.

This film does not name a killer. Rev. Lyn George Kelly was charged, tried twice and acquitted. Frank and Albert Jones were never charged. Everyone named here is presumed innocent of these murders. A newspaper report is not proof.

A note on one date: the inquest summary we used records the coroner asking about "Sunday morning." The girls stayed over Sunday night and were expected home before school, so we read it as Monday, June 10. The sources do not agree, and the film says so on screen.
